# Runlith licensing

Runlith is **source-available**, not OSI Open Source. The repository's default
license is [PolyForm Shield 1.0.0](LICENSE). That license text governs; this
page explains scope and product direction and does not add restrictions to or
replace its grants.

## Current scope

Unless a file or component explicitly states otherwise, Runlith-owned code and
documentation in this repository are offered under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0.
Third-party code, dependencies and assets retain their respective licenses and
notices; the root license does not relicense them.

Shield permits use, modification and distribution for permitted purposes,
subject to its notices and other conditions. Its noncompete restriction applies
to providing products that compete with the software or products the licensor
or its affiliates provide using the software, including free competing products.
Read the license's Competition, New Products and Discontinued Products sections
for the precise boundaries.

Using Runlith for ordinary internal development or to build non-competing
commercial software is distinct from distributing a competing Runlith product.
Using the tool does not by itself place independently authored user projects
under Shield. Copying licensed Runlith implementation into an output or another
product is a separate licensing question.

## Commercial products and OEM rights

Pro Local, Pro Subscription, Teams and Enterprise are the product direction
defined in the [monetization strategy](AI_ENGINEERING_OS_MONETIZATION_AND_PLUGIN_ECOSYSTEM.md).
Their purchase terms and any additional commercially licensed components must
be explicitly identified when offered. The current Shield grant does not by
itself enforce paid feature entitlements or a per-seat subscription.

Rights beyond Shield, including otherwise prohibited competitive distribution,
OEM, white-label or hosted offerings, require a separate agreement from the
relevant rights holder. Not every modification, redistribution or embedding
requires such an agreement: uses already permitted by Shield remain permitted.
Buying Pro does not automatically grant OEM rights.

This repository does not yet contain an executed or counsel-approved commercial
license or EULA. This page is not one. Future commercial terms must not purport
to withdraw rights already granted under Shield. The intended Pro Local offer
is perpetual use of the last eligible version after its update period ends;
that entitlement must be specified in the actual purchase agreement.

## Plugin SDK and Apache-2.0

The selected direction for a future independently identified Plugin SDK,
associated public schemas and examples is
[Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
No component is designated Apache-2.0 by this page. There is currently no
separately licensed Plugin SDK in this workspace; internal IPC, execution and
planner contracts remain under the repository default license unless explicitly
marked otherwise.

An eventual SDK must carry its own license and explicit scope. Apache-2.0 for
an SDK will not provide an alternative license for the Runlith core. Supporting
independent paid plugins is product strategy, not a blanket exception to
Shield's noncompete provisions. Plugin/core licensing boundaries and any
necessary additional permissions must be settled before opening the ecosystem.

## Contributions, trademarks and release readiness

Before accepting substantial external contributions, establish contributor
terms sufficient for the intended Community and commercial licensing model.
A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) alone should not be treated as an
additional grant of commercial relicensing rights. No CLA is established here.

Source-code availability does not imply permission to claim official Runlith
endorsement. A separate trademark policy remains to be established; this page
does not assert a trademark registration or invent a legal owner.

Before public distribution, obtain qualified legal review of copyright
ownership and contributor permissions, third-party notices, commercial
agreements, and plugin/trademark boundaries. Adding the standard license text
is not evidence that those release checks have been completed.
