## What Apache-2.0 allows

Everyone — individuals, businesses of any size, and governments — may
use, modify, and distribute Runlith, including for commercial and
production purposes, subject to the license conditions: keep the
`LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, mark modified files, and retain copyright
and attribution notices (see `LICENSE` sections 4–5). Each contributor
also grants a patent license for their contributions (section 3), which
terminates only if you sue over patent infringement by the Work.

## Contributions

Contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in the Work are
under Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE` section 5, inbound = outbound). No separate
Contributor License Agreement (CLA) is established; a Developer
Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off on commits is welcome but not a
substitute for counsel-approved terms if the project later needs them.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
